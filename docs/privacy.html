Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026

Lume is a daily glow coach. It is built so your face stays on your phone.

What we collect

On this device: a photo you take for a scan, the glow scores computed from it, the onboarding answers you tap, ritual checkmarks, and whether a trial is running. That lives in on-device storage. It is not an account. It does not sync.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan. You can refuse the permission. Photos are not sent to a server as images.

Optional coaching lines

If insights are requested, Lume may send numeric scores and the onboarding answers (goal, concerns, skin type, sleep, age range) to generate three short lines. The photograph itself is not attached.

What we do not do

No advertising identifier. No face database. No sale of data. No tracking across other companies’ apps. No account, so there is no profile to hand to a broker.

Subscriptions

When Lume ships on the App Store, Apple processes payment. We receive subscription status, not your card. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Children

Lume is not directed at children under 13.

Your controls

Delete my data, in the app, wipes scans, scores, the ritual log, and the trial on that device. Deleting the app does the same for that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
