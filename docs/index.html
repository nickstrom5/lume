Questions

Where do my photos go?

They stay on this device. The picture is not uploaded. Optional coaching lines are written from scores and the answers you tapped, never from storing your face.

How do I cancel?

Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends or it renews.

Is this medical?

No. Lume is a sixty-second coach with a camera. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a clinician.
