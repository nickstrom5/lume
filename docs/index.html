FAQ

Where do my photos go?

They stay on this iPhone. Lume does not upload the picture.

How do I cancel?

Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends or Apple will renew.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. The face stays on one screen and the ritual on the other. Regular iPhones stack them.
