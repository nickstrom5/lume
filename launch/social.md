## The rule

Don’t invent formats. Skincare already has them. Copy the format, change the story, post at volume.

| Tier | Signal | If stuck |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Low views | Hook / first frame. |
| 1 | Views | Product appears too late. Score or ritual in the first 2 seconds. |
| 2 | Views + comments | Entertaining but not the app. Make the scan the story. |
| 3 | + Downloads | Onboarding / paywall. Marketing did its job. |
| 4 | + Trials | Scale this concept. |
| 5 | + Low churn | Do more of exactly this. |

Rules for every video:
- Product on screen in 2 seconds. No intro, no logo sting.
- Text hook on frame one, 6 words or fewer.
- 7–15 seconds.
- CTA is the name on screen at the end. Don’t say “link in bio”.
- Captions on.
- One line in the caption: `Coaching tool, not a diagnosis. Photos stay on the phone.`

## First 10 videos (record in one afternoon)

Phone on a tripod, bathroom window, same hour you’ll actually scan. Second phone records.

### 1. The demo
**Hook:** `One photo. A number.`
Shots: open Lume → camera → face in the window light → 78 appears → “not a diagnosis” on screen → sixty-second list: rinse, press, seal, SPF.

### 2. Sixty seconds, timed
**Hook:** `A skincare routine that fits.`
Film the AM ritual with a timer overlay hitting 0:60. End on the checked list.

### 3. Same window, same hour
**Hook:** `Stop scanning in five different lights.`
Three bad scans (overhead, flashlight, car) then one by the window. Score only moves when the light is the same.

### 4. Slideshow: 5 signs
**Hook:** `5 signs your routine is too long`
1. You own 11 serums and use 2.
2. You skip SPF “because you’re inside”.
3. You film GRWM and never wash the brushes.
4. You change products every time a reel tells you to.
5. Slide 5: Lume, sixty seconds. Same window.

### 5. iPhone Duo
**Hook:** `Face on the left. Ritual on the right.`
Open Lume on Duo (or the desktop dual-pane). Don’t explain. Show the book.

### 6. SPF even inside
**Hook:** `Windows count.`
One sentence. Check the SPF step. End card.

### 7. Founder, day 1
**Hook:** `Day 1 of a 60-second face`
Talking head, phone in hand. “I built a glow coach that takes one photo and sixty seconds. Photos stay on the phone. Day 1: 0 scans besides mine.” Post a number every day until 1,000.

### 8. Share card
**Hook:** `The only selfie I’d post`
Generate a share card in the app. Hold it. Don’t over-caption.

### 9. What we don’t do
**Hook:** `This is not a dermatologist`
On-screen: no diagnosis, no treatment, no face uploaded. Then the scan. Honesty converts in this category because everyone else lies.

### 10. Duo launch week (post Oct 23)
**Hook:** `The Duo already knows your face`
Book layout, one sentence: “You just haven’t asked it.”

Record 1–9 before TestFlight. Post 1–2 a day from two accounts if you want (brand + personal). Personal can stitch the brand demo. Brand never stitches personal memes.
